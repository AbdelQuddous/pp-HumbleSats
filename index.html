Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Summary

Private by design. No account. No ads. No behavioral tracking. Your stack is stored locally. We never upload your Bitcoin holdings, notes, or goals to any server. Live prices and Pro purchases require a network connection.

HumbleSats does not collect analytics, does not use a tracking SDK, does not generate anonymous user IDs, and does not require an account. The app contacts Bitcoin price providers for live prices and the platform's own store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) for optional in-app purchases. No third-party purchase service is used.

1. Data We Store Locally

HumbleSats stores the following data on your device only in the app's local storage:

  • Stack entries: Bitcoin amounts, fiat amounts, dates, and optional notes for each purchase you log.
  • Goal data: Your Bitcoin accumulation target and milestone progress.
  • App settings: Theme preference, display unit (sats/BTC), Humble Mode toggle, biometric lock setting.
  • Purchase state: Whether HumbleSats Pro has been unlocked (cached locally for offline access; verified via the store when online).

This data is stored in a local SQLite database in the app's private storage. Android backup is explicitly disabled to prevent your holdings from being copied to Google Drive or a connected computer. On iOS, HumbleSats explicitly excludes its local database from iCloud and computer backups.

Your portfolio data never leaves your device through the app's own code. The only way your portfolio data leaves the device is if you explicitly use the Export feature to create a backup file and then share or upload that file yourself.

2. Data We Do NOT Collect

HumbleSats does not collect, transmit, or store on any server:

  • Your Bitcoin holdings or amounts
  • Your fiat investment amounts
  • Your purchase dates or transaction history
  • Your notes or labels
  • Your goal targets or progress
  • Your wallet addresses or private keys
  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your device identifiers
  • Your location

HumbleSats has no account system. There is no login, no sign-up, and no way for us to identify you. There is no analytics SDK, no behavioral tracking library, no anonymous user ID generation, and no usage telemetry of any kind in the app. The app does not use Firebase, Google Analytics, RevenueCat, or any other third-party analytics or purchase SDK.

3. Network Connections

HumbleSats uses only the following categories of network connection:

Connection Purpose What is sent Required?
Bitcoin price providers (mempool.space, CoinGecko, Coinbase, and Blockchain.com) Fetch the current BTC price and the latest block height for display and calculations No portfolio data, account data, or app-generated identifier. As with any HTTPS request, the provider can receive standard network metadata such as an IP address. Yes (core feature)
Apple App Store / Google Play Billing Process and verify HumbleSats Pro purchase Purchase receipt (handled by Apple/Google directly) Yes (for Pro purchase only)

No analytics service. No crash reporting SDK. No advertising network. No tracking pixels. No data brokers. No third-party purchase service. The app does not upload portfolio data to any server.

4. No Analytics, No Behavioral Tracking

HumbleSats contains no analytics and no behavioral tracking. There is no Firebase, no Google Analytics, no RevenueCat, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Sentry, no crash reporting, and no usage telemetry of any kind in the app.

We do not know how many times you've opened the app. We do not know which features you use. We do not know your screen flow. We do not track your behavior. This is a deliberate design choice, not an oversight.

5. Third-Party Services

Apple App Store / Google Play Store

In-app purchases are processed by Apple and Google using their native billing systems (StoreKit on iOS, Google Play Billing on Android). HumbleSats uses Flutter's official in_app_purchase plugin to talk directly to the platform store; it does not use a third-party purchase service. We do not process or store payment information. We rely on the store-reported purchase result to unlock Pro locally and do not operate a receipt-validation server. We never see your payment details, and no anonymous user ID is generated or transmitted by HumbleSats.

Bitcoin Price API

HumbleSats fetches the current Bitcoin price and the latest block height from public providers (mempool.space first, with CoinGecko, Coinbase, and Blockchain.com as fallbacks). The request contains no portfolio data, account data, or app-generated identifier. Like any HTTPS request, a provider can receive standard network metadata such as an IP address.

6. Data Security

Your financial data is stored in a local SQLite database inside the app's private sandbox on your device. On iOS, this is protected by the iOS app sandbox. On Android, this is protected by Android's app sandbox.

Backups are excluded. On Android, the app sets android:allowBackup="false" and configures data extraction rules to exclude all app data from cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. On iOS, the local database is explicitly marked as excluded from iCloud and computer backups.

HumbleSats includes an optional biometric lock, free for everyone, that requires your fingerprint, face unlock, or device PIN to open the app. This is an additional layer of protection on top of your device's existing lock screen.

All network connections use HTTPS (TLS encryption).

7. Data Deletion

You can delete all your data at any time by going to Settings → Delete all data. This permanently erases every stack, goal, and setting on your device. There is no cloud copy — once deleted, the data is gone.

Uninstalling the app also deletes all local data. Purchase records remain with Apple/Google (your store account, not ours) so you can restore Pro after reinstalling.

8. Export and Backup

You can export your data as a password-encrypted backup (AES-256-GCM), as plain JSON (full backup), or as CSV (spreadsheet) at any time via Settings → Export data. The encryption password never leaves your device and cannot be recovered. The exported file is saved to your device's file system. You are responsible for where you store or share this file.

You can restore from a JSON export file via Settings → Restore from export. This replaces all data on the device with the contents of the file.

You can also import purchase history from a CSV file you choose (for example an export from Kraken, Coinbase, or River) via Settings → Import history. The file is read on your device only and is never uploaded.

9. Children's Privacy

HumbleSats is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect any data from children. The app is rated "Everyone" on Google Play.

10. Your Rights

HumbleSats does not collect your personal data on any server. The only data processed is purchase receipts, which are handled directly by Apple/Google through their own billing systems. You can manage your purchase history through your Apple ID or Google Account settings.

You have full control over your Bitcoin data on your device at all times. You can export it, delete it, or uninstall the app to remove it entirely.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, if appropriate, notify you within the app. We will never add analytics, behavioral tracking, or data collection to HumbleSats without updating this policy and notifying users.

12. Verifying These Claims

The network connections and local storage behavior are implemented exactly as described here. There are no hidden SDKs, no obfuscated tracking calls, and no background data collection. The app's source code is not public at this time, but you can confirm its behavior with any network monitor: HumbleSats only connects to the price providers and the platform store listed in Section 3.
