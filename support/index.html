Support

Private by design. No account required.

Contact

Email abdquddos@gmail.com. You can also use Settings → Contact support in the app, which fills in your app version for you.

Never send your seed phrase, private keys, or exchange passwords. HumbleSats will never ask for them.

Restore HumbleSats Pro

Open Settings → Restore purchases while signed in to the same Google account that bought Pro. Pro is tied to your store account, not your device, so it comes back after reinstalling or switching phones.

Refunds

Purchases are processed by Google Play, and refunds follow Google Play's refund policy. Within 48 hours of purchase you can usually request a refund directly from Google Play. After that, email us and we will help.

Back up your stack

Your data lives only on your device and is excluded from cloud backups on purpose. Use Settings → Export data to create a JSON backup, and keep that file somewhere you control.

Move to a new phone

Export a JSON backup on the old phone, transfer the file privately, then use Settings → Restore from export on the new phone. Restore Pro separately with Restore purchases.

Prices look wrong or stale

HumbleSats shows when a price was last updated. Prices come from public providers (CoinGecko, Coinbase, Blockchain.com) and need an internet connection. If one provider is down, the app falls back to another. Pull to refresh on the home screen, or check Settings → Bitcoin price data.
